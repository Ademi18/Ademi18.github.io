Power BI · TS Academy Capstone

FleetOps Logistics — Operations Analytics Report (2022–2024)

My TS Academy capstone: a full Power BI build analyzing a 14-table logistics dataset spanning 85,410 trips, 122M miles, and $299M in revenue across 150 drivers, 120 trucks, and 200 customers (2022–2024). Built the complete data model, all DAX measures, and an 11-chart dashboard with 8 KPI cards and 5 interactive slicers.

  • Diagnosed a 22-point gap between pickup (66.7%) and delivery (44.6%) on-time rates, pointing to a last-mile detention bottleneck
  • Found route yield varies 2.1x between top and bottom corridors (Philadelphia–NY at $3.69/mi vs. Charlotte–Denver at $1.70/mi)
  • Quantified gross margin improvement from 62.7% to 65.18%, driven primarily by a $4.3M fuel-cost saving
Revenue Opportunity

Recommended systematic detention billing automation, projected to recover $2–5M annually in currently unbilled costs.

Safety Insight

Equipment damage ($741K) is the leading safety cost, with incidents up 37.6% year-over-year.

Load Mix Insight

Shifting 10% of volume to high-yield short-haul lanes projected to add $1.5–2.5M in revenue with no new assets.

Tools: Power BI: Data Modeling, Power Query, DAX, KPI Cards, Slicers, Multi-Chart Dashboard Design
Excel · SQL · DecodeLabs Internship

E-Commerce Sales Performance Dashboard

A three-week internship project: cleaned and queried a 1,200-order retail dataset with SQL (filtering, aggregation, grouping), then designed an interactive Excel dashboard to communicate performance across products, payment methods, referral sources, coupons, and order status.

  • KPIs: $1,264,762 total revenue, 1,200 orders, $1,054 average order value, 3,535 units sold
  • Revenue by Product, Monthly Sales Trend, Payment Method split, Referral Source Performance, Coupon Analysis, and Order Status breakdown
  • Product, Payment Method, Order Status, and Coupon Code slicers for interactive filtering
Fulfillment Red Flag

Cancelled orders (250) outnumbered successfully Delivered orders (231), a checkout or fulfillment issue worth investigating.

Referral Insight

Instagram drove the highest referral revenue at $275,285, ahead of Email, Google, and Facebook.

Coupon Insight

FREESHIP was the most-used coupon (313 redemptions), narrowly ahead of orders with no coupon at all (309).

Tools: SQL (SSMS): Filtering, Aggregation, Grouping · Excel: Pivot Tables, KPI Cards, Charts, Slicers
