Projects

01
Microsoft Excel

Sales Analysis Dashboard

Project Overview

Built a comprehensive multi-chart sales analysis dashboard in Microsoft Excel, combining data cleaning, KPI calculation, pivot table analysis, and interactive slicers into a single unified view. The dashboard enables stakeholders to explore performance across products, sales representatives, cities, and time — filterable by region and product category.

What was delivered

  • Data quality audit and cleaning verification
  • KPIs calculated: Revenue (₦465.67M), COGS, Profit, and Monthly Customers (2,098)
  • Horizontal bar chart — Product by Profit (Laptop A13 most profitable at ₦105.3M)
  • Bar chart — Sales Rep by Revenue (Peter leading at ₦434.8M)
  • Pie chart — City by COGS (Port Harcourt highest at ₦493.8M)
  • Line chart — Monthly Customers by Retail vs Corporate (May showing sharp drop)
  • Region and Category slicers for interactive filtering
Key Finding

Laptop A13 generated the highest profit (₦105.3M) — nearly double the second-placed product, Sofa Classic.

Insight

May saw a dramatic collapse in both Retail and Corporate customer numbers, signalling a seasonal or operational disruption worth investigating.

Sales Rep Analysis

Peter outperformed all other sales reps with ₦434.8M in revenue — a 15% lead over the next best performer, Musa.

Tools Used

Microsoft Excel — Pivot Tables, KPI Cards, Charts, Slicers, Dashboard Layout

02
Power BI

Sales Performance Dashboard

Project Overview

Designed and built an end-to-end Power BI dashboard covering January to March 2023 sales data. The project involved multi-table data cleaning, table joins, DAX-based KPI calculations, and a suite of visualisations — all wired to interactive Region and Gender slicers. The dashboard surfaces brand performance, customer demographics, purchase behaviour by colour, and income-level profitability in a single view.

What was delivered

  • Column-by-column data cleaning and table join across multiple tables
  • KPIs: Total Revenue (₦3.11M), Total Cost (₦2.17M), Profit (₦931.91K), Customers (250)
  • Bar chart — Total Profit by Brand (Apple #1 at ₦197K)
  • Bar chart — Total Purchase by Color (White leads at ₦1.22M)
  • Bar chart — Total Revenue by Year (2023 baseline established)
  • Line/bar chart — Total Customers by Month (February dip identified)
  • Donut chart — Sum of Profit by Income Level (Medium 37.23%, Low 32.43%, High 30.34%)
  • Region and Gender slicers; key insight callouts embedded in the sidebar
Key Finding

Apple products drove the highest profit (₦197K) — 23% more than second-place brands Lenovo and Samsung.

Customer Insight

164 of 250 customers (65.6%) are female — suggesting a strong female buyer base that should inform future marketing strategy.

Insight

Electronics peaked in February while overall sales dipped — a nuanced finding only visible when filtering by category.

Tools Used

Power BI — Power Query, Table Joins, DAX, KPI Cards, Slicers, Donut & Bar Charts
